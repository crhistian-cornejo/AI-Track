- Plugins (macOS): on a Mac with a broken plugin installed, scanning no longer opens several "AI-Track" icons in the Dock, each with the plugin's dialog. The scanner stays in the background and the plugin's message appears in the scan report.
- New instrument, Drum Synth: nine synthetic drum types (kick, snare, rimshot, hi-hat, clap, tom, percussion, cymbal, FX) with 26 engines, kit or mono mode, the closed hi-hat chokes the open one, 43 presets.
- New clip type, Step Sequencer (Cmd+Shift+P on an instrument track): rows with their own length, rate, direction and swing; steps with velocity, chance and repeat; converts to notes and back.
- Racks: Group and Ungroup keep macro and modulator mappings, folding a device no longer removes it, a new rack comes with an empty chain, and racks can be saved as presets and found in Cmd+K, with four factory racks.
- New modulator, Shaper: draw a curve and send it to up to eight parameters (pumping, gates, rises), with presets.
- Auto Filter: DJ, Comb, Vowel, Morph, Resample and Notch+LP types, Wander and Ramp LFO shapes, and a sidechain envelope follower. Existing projects sound the same.
- Chord: Strum, Chance per voice and Learn.
- Fixes: no crash with audio devices below 40 kHz; frozen tracks keep their render when the project folder moves; a crash while recording audio no longer leaves an empty take; a new track lands after a nested group's tracks; a failed undo no longer drops the step; a loop recording over a step clip is no longer lost.
